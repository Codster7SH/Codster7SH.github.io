Hi again, I'm Shivam Pandey.

I'm doing a PhD building explainable AI algorithms and a remote, IoMT-enabled tech pipeline for elderly care. I hail from the north-central part of India and grew up in my home city, Lucknow, before spending a few years living near the lakes and mountains close to the Shivalik ranges, and later the national capital. After wandering through technology and business schools, and living in different places, at 28, I now live in Bergen, Norway.

How to pronounce my name

shee-vum paan-day

(Shivam means "the auspicious one" — though mostly it just means I get called "Shiva" by spellcheckers and avid readers.)

Fun facts

For work, I

  • Am doing a PhD at UiB (Faculty of Medicine), where I'm working on building a knowledge-based, explainable fuzzy-AI model and designing a fuzzy-enabled data pipeline for an IoMT app-based ecosystem — helping older adults and people with chronic conditions live comfortably and independently at home.
  • Explore fuzzy logic, real-time systems, and generative models to make complex medical data actually make sense to humans.
  • Experiment with computer vision, image analysis, and augmented reality in my free time — including past projects on brain tumor analysis and fracture detection (code is on my GitHub).
  • Do academic things — you can check out my publications on my Publications page.

A recent poem I drafted while sitting by my window

With the drizzle of drops
At midnight where the lights pop

With the music,
With the view,
My window makes sure
I get through.

The droplets beat
In time with my playlist on repeat
In the lap of nature
I forget the future.

Make it last,
Make it longer
Drive the night past,
Hold it a little stronger.

Keep the moment,
Face the mirror,
Find yourself,
Forget the incompletes, inconsistent, and the fear.
